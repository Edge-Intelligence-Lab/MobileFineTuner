An on-device supervisor samples resources approximately every3 seconds and
stops its own child on RSS above5 GiB, available memory below1500 MiB, battery

not chip temperature, and supervised duration includes monitoring/I/O overhead.
Storage formats differ across cases; the memory numbers are descriptive and
not a controlled claim that these switches always reduce peak RSS.

## Strict adapter diagnostic: not passed

Printed losses above have limited precision and do not establish numerical
identity. All independent cases begin with the same settings and initial
adapter seed. Comparing their final1,352,832 adapter coordinates gives:

| Comparison | Maximum absolute adapter difference | Coordinates exceeding2e-5 |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Native BF16 ordinary vs FP32 ordinary | 5.2316114e-5 | 4 |
| FP32 ordinary vs FP32 combined | 1.3547507e-4 | 9 |
| Native BF16 ordinary vs FP32 combined | 1.5303912e-4 | Not used as a matched-storage comparison |

The matched-FP32 comparison **does not meet the existing strict2e-5 per-coordinate
adapter diagnostic bound**. It is not relabeled as a numerical-equivalence pass,
and no tolerance, optimizer epsilon or training setting is changed to do so.
The execution/snapshot checks above remain distinct from this failed diagnostic.

Different storage/kernel/reduction paths and Adam sensitivity to near-zero
gradients are relevant possibilities, as in earlier host diagnostics; this phone
check does not independently isolate the source of every difference. It does
not establish full-size cross-framework Adam trajectory parity or unchanged
model accuracy. Host HF gradient/SGD checks retain their separate source/scope.
